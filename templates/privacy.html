Privacy Policy

MiraNex is a project built for academic purposes as part of a college course. This page explains what information the site collects, how it is used, and how you can control it.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your username, email address, and password. Your password is never stored in plain text. It is hashed using a secure hashing method before being saved.

As you use the site, we also store:

  • Anime you add to your Watchlist or Watched list
  • Comments you post on anime pages

We do not collect payment information, location data, or any information beyond what is described above.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to:

  • Log you in and keep you signed in during your visit
  • Show your Watchlist and Watched pages
  • Display your username next to comments you post

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with any third party.

MiraNex uses a single session cookie to keep you logged in while you browse the site. This cookie does not track your activity across other websites, and no advertising or analytics cookies are used.

Any comment you post, along with your username, is visible to anyone who views that anime's page. Please avoid sharing personal information in your comments.

You may permanently delete your account at any time from your profile menu. Deleting your account immediately and permanently removes your account, Watchlist, Watched list, and all comments you have posted. This action cannot be undone.

Your data is stored only for as long as your account exists. Once your account is deleted, all associated data is removed immediately.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out at miranex15@gmail.com

A Note on This Project

MiraNex is an academic project developed as part of a college assignment. This Privacy Policy describes how the application actually handles data, but it is not a formal legal document.
